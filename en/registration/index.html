What to bring

  • Whiteboard
  • Markers
  • Notebooks and pencils
  • Bag
  • Completed homework sheets

Students must bring the required materials to attend class. Students without them will not be permitted to attend.

Registration information preview

The Maktab has confirmed that future registration will include school year level, address and parent or guardian information. The list below is a proposed preview, not a final list of required fields.

  • Student’s name
  • Student’s age or date of birth (exact requirement awaiting confirmation)
  • School year level, for example Year 4
  • Address
  • Parent or guardian’s name (for children)
  • Parent or guardian’s contact details (for children)
