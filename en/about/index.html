About us

Maktab Alzahra SA is an Islamic educational and community organisation in Adelaide, South Australia. We offer free Quran classes for all ages, with registration subject to Maktab staff approval.

Madinah al-Munawwarah

Quran education

Quran classes are free for all ages. All registrations require approval by Maktab staff. Classes are on Saturday and Sunday; students should arrive by 9:40 am and classroom doors close at 10:00 am.

Our Adelaide community

Maktab Alzahra SA is based in Adelaide, South Australia. This website brings together information about Quran education and ways to support the Maktab.

Ways to get involved

You can read about volunteering and financial support on this website. Volunteer applications are not currently open; donations are accepted by bank transfer.

Legal name

Australian Youths Alliance inco

Maktab Alzahra SA is the name our community knows us by. Our legal name, which you will see on bank transfers and official documents, is Australian Youths Alliance inco.
