Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Baba Bricklaying Pty Ltd, trading as Baba Bricklayer (ABN 32 649 863 838), collects through this website, why we collect it and what we do with it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you send a quote request we collect the details you type into the form: your name, phone number, email address, and, if you give them, your suburb, the type of job and a description of the work. If you phone or email us, we keep the details you give us so we can reply.

Why we collect it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, arrange and carry out the work, and contact you about that job. We do not use your details for advertising, and we do not sell or rent them to anyone.

Where it is kept

Quote requests are stored in this website's database and sent to our email inbox. The website is hosted by a third-party hosting company, and our email is provided by a third-party email service, so your details are held on their systems on our behalf. Some of these systems may be located outside Australia.

Who we share it with

We only share your details when it is needed to do the job, for example with a builder or supplier involved in your project, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and other services

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not load fonts or scripts from other companies. Links to other websites, including our Google reviews page, are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and job details for as long as we need them to do the work and to meet our legal and tax record-keeping obligations. You can ask us to delete an enquiry that did not go ahead.

Seeing or correcting your details

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. If you are still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
