Questions

About brick garages,answered.

Something else on your mind? Call us on 0449 824 153.

Do you build from my plans?

Yes. We work from your approved plans, whether they come from your builder, your designer or your engineer.

Can the brick match my house?

We help you choose a brick and mortar as close as possible to the existing house, or one that deliberately contrasts if you prefer that.

Do you organise council approval?

Approval is normally arranged by you or your builder before work starts. We can tell you what we need from the plans to give you a quote.
