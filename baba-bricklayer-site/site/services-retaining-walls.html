Questions

About retaining walls,answered.

Something else on your mind? Call us on 0449 824 153.

Does my retaining wall need an engineer?

Taller walls and walls carrying extra load usually need an engineer's design and council approval. We will tell you early if we think yours does.

Brick or block: which is better?

Concrete block is the usual choice for strength and cost. Brick, or block with a brick or render finish, suits walls that are on show.

Can you replace a wall that is failing?

Yes. We take down the old wall and rebuild it properly, with the reinforcement it needs.
