Questions

About brick fences,answered.

Something else on your mind? Call us on 0449 824 153.

Can you combine brick with steel or timber?

Yes. Brick piers with steel or timber infill panels are one of the most common fences we build.

Can the fence match my house?

We choose brick and mortar to suit the house, so the fence looks like part of the home.

Do I need council approval?

It depends on the height and where the fence sits. Check with your council before work starts. We can tell you the fence height and details they are likely to ask about.
