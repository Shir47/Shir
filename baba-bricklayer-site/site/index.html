Know the signs

Small cracks.Worth a closer look.

Brickwork usually gives a warning before it becomes a bigger job. These are the common ones.

A general guide only. Have a bricklayer look at the wall before deciding on repairs.

  1. 01

    Cracks in the bricks or mortar

    Hairline or stepped cracks let water in and tend to grow over time.

  2. 02

    Crumbling or missing mortar

    Joints that have worn back leave the bricks without proper support.

  3. 03

    Loose or shifting bricks

    Bricks that move when touched are no longer bonded to the wall.

  4. 04

    A wall that leans or bulges

    Movement in the wall itself should be looked at sooner rather than later.

  5. 05

    Damp patches or white salt stains

    Moisture is getting into the brickwork and bringing salts to the surface.
