What we build, fix and take away

New work and old work, homes and commercial sites. If it is made of brick or block, we can lay it, repair it or remove it.

  • Brick repairs and restorations

    Cracked, loose or weathered bricks replaced and joints repointed, matched to the wall you already have.

  • Brick garages

    Garages built in brick, attached or freestanding, to sit with the rest of the house.

  • Brick fences

    Front fences, piers and letterboxes.

  • Brick removal

    Walls and brickwork taken down cleanly.

  • Brick and block retaining walls

    Reinforced walls that hold back the slope and tidy up a sloping block.

  • Brickwork maintenance

    Regular upkeep that catches small problems in mortar and brick before they turn into big ones.

Twenty-five years, one brick at a time

We have spent more than two decades laying brick around Adelaide, from detailed face brickwork to the structural repairs nobody sees. More than 800 finished projects later, most of our work still comes from people who were happy with the last job.

Choose us and you get a team that turns up, works safely, and leaves a wall that is plumb, level and built to stay that way.

  • Safety first. Strict site safety for our team, your family and your property on every job.
  • Finished on time. A clear timeline at the start, and steady work to meet it.
  • Current methods. Up-to-date tools and techniques, used by people who know the old ones too.
  • Current designs. Bonds, bricks and finishes that suit how homes are being built now.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

A wide range of masonry work: new construction, renovations, repairs and restoration, for residential, commercial and industrial properties.

How experienced is your team?

Between us we have decades of experience in bricklaying and masonry, and we keep our training up to date.

How do you keep the site safe?

We follow strict safety procedures, inspect the site regularly, keep the team trained and use well-maintained equipment, so the site stays safe for everyone on and around it.

Can I see examples or references?

Yes. There are photos on this page, and we can provide references when you ask.

How long will my job take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the job. We give you an estimate and a timeline at the first consultation, then work to finish on time without cutting corners.
