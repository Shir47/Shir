What we do

New work and old work, homes and commercial sites. If it is made of brick or block, we can lay it, repair it or remove it.

  • Repairs and restoration

    Cracked, loose or weathered bricks replaced and joints repointed, matched to the wall you already have.

  • Brick garages

    Attached or freestanding garages, built in brick to sit with the rest of the house.

  • Brick fences

    Front fences, piers and letterboxes that give the street something to look at.

  • Retaining walls

    Reinforced brick and block walls that hold back the slope and tidy up a sloping block.

  • Brick removal

    Walls and brickwork taken down cleanly, with the site left ready for what comes next.

  • Maintenance

    Regular upkeep that catches small problems in mortar and brick before they turn into big ones.

We also take on new construction, renovations and remodelling. Tell us about your job.
