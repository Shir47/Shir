Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't see your question here? Call us on 0449 824 153 and we will be glad to help.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

A wide range of masonry work: new construction, renovations, repairs and restoration, for residential, commercial and industrial properties.

How experienced is your team?

Between us we have decades of experience in bricklaying and masonry, and we keep our training up to date.

How do you keep the site safe?

We follow strict safety procedures, inspect the site regularly, keep the team trained and use well-maintained equipment, so the site stays safe for everyone on and around it.

Can I see examples or references?

Yes. You can see photos on our projects page, and we can provide references when you ask.

How long will my job take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the job. We give you an estimate and a timeline at the first consultation, then work to finish on time without cutting corners.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the quote form or call us on 0449 824 153. We will talk the job through and answer any questions.
