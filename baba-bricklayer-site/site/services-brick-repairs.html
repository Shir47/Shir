What is included

Brick repairs and restorations, done properly.

Most damaged brickwork can be repaired without rebuilding the wall. We cut out what has failed, replace it with matching brick and mortar, and leave the repair as hard to spot as we can make it.

  • Cracked, broken and loose bricks cut out and replaced
  • Worn mortar raked out and repointed
  • Brick and mortar colour matched to the existing wall
  • Damaged corners, sills and window heads rebuilt
  • Older and heritage-style brickwork restored with care

    Cracks have appeared

Questions

About brick repairs,answered.

Something else on your mind? Call us on 0449 824 153.

Can you match my existing bricks?

We match the brick and the mortar colour as closely as we can. With older bricks an exact match is not always possible, so we show you the options before we start.

Does the whole wall need rebuilding?

Usually not. In most cases only the damaged section needs to be cut out and replaced. We will tell you honestly if a repair will not last.

What causes brickwork to crack?

Common causes are ground movement, moisture and age. We look at the wall and tell you what we think is behind it, so the repair deals with the cause and not only the crack.
